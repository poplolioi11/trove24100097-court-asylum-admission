# 兇入癲院（《東華新報》1901-07-03 第2版，澳洲國家圖書館） / Xiong ru dian yuan [A Court-Ordered Admission to an Asylum]: Transcription Dataset

《東華新報》1901-07-03 刊載的〈兇入癲院〉。接續本報前報，記德皇遇襲案審理與涉案者因被認定失常而送入癲人院監置。報刊所用法律及病名詞彙照錄。

Xiong ru dian yuan [A Court-Ordered Admission to an Asylum] appeared in Tung Wah News (1901-07-03). Following an earlier notice, the article reports the court hearing after the assault on the German emperor and the accused's confinement in an asylum after being deemed of unsound mind. The transcription retains the contemporary legal and psychiatric wording.
